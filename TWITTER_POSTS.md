## Engagement Tactics for Twitter

### Reply Strategies:
- **Ask follow-up questions** in replies to continue conversations
- **Share additional insights** not in the original tweet
- **Thank people personally** who share their own experiences
- **Add context** when people misunderstand your point

### Retweet with Comments:
- Share relevant educational content with your take
- Comment on trending topics with your expertise
- Add insights to other developers' technical posts

### Twitter Spaces Topics:
- "The future of AI in education"
- "Building apps that matter"
- "Scaling mobile apps: war stories"
- "Why most edtech fails (and how to fix it)"

### Hashtag Strategy:
**Primary:** #EdTech #AppDev #AI #StartupLife #MobileDev
**Secondary:** #Flutter #Firebase #IndieHacker #BuildInPublic #TechStartup
**Specific:** #MathEducation #AITutor #StudentLife #ParentLife

### Best Posting Times:
- **Weekdays:** 9-10 AM, 12-1 PM, 5-6 PM EST
- **Weekends:** 10-11 AM EST
- **Avoid:** Late evenings, very early mornings

### Content Calendar Suggestions:
- **Monday:** Motivation/Week starter posts
- **Tuesday-Thursday:** Technical insights, user stories
- **Friday:** Lessons learned, week wrap-ups
- **Saturday:** Behind-the-scenes, personal stories
- **Sunday:** Vision/future-focused content

### Visual Content Ideas:
- Screenshots of the app in action
- Before/after problem solving examples
- User testimonials as quote cards
- Simple infographics about math anxiety stats
- Behind-the-scenes development photos
- Metrics and growth charts
- Architecture diagrams (for technical audience)

### Engagement Boosters:
- **Polls:** "What's your biggest math anxiety trigger?"
- **Questions:** "What subject made you want to throw your textbook?"
- **Fill-in-the-blank:** "The hardest part about learning math is ____"
- **This or That:** "Learning style: Visual step-by-step OR audio explanations?"
